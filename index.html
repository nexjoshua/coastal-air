Why install a modern system?

If you're relying on fans, portable units or old heaters, a new reverse-cycle system can make a big difference all year round.

Heats and cools

Reverse-cycle air conditioning is one of the most energy-efficient ways to heat a home, and it cools too.

Inverter technology

Modern inverter systems adjust their speed instead of switching on and off, so they run quieter and steadier.

Smart control

Many new systems can be controlled from your phone, so you come home to the right temperature.

Room-by-room comfort

Ducted zoning or multiple split systems let you heat or cool only the rooms you use.

Do you offer any warranties on your work?

Yes, we offer a warranty on all labour and parts provided by our company. Specific warranty terms depend on the service and parts used, but typically range from 1 to 5 years.

What qualifies as an electrical emergency?

Exposed wires, smoke from an electrical device, loss of power, an electrical fire, or any situation where there's a risk of electric shock. Call us immediately, or dial 000 if it involves fire or life-threatening conditions.

Do you install smart home devices?

Yes. We install a range of smart home devices, including thermostats, security systems and lighting that can be controlled from your phone or by voice.
